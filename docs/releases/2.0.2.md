# More of Dots 2.0.2

- Replays are ready to seek about four times sooner. A 24-minute replay now simulates in about 8 seconds, down from 34.
- Simulated replays are kept in memory and no longer written to disk. A 24-minute replay takes about 36 MB, down from 1.2 GB of intermediate data. Startup removes the simulation cache left by earlier versions.
- Added 16x playback speed.
- The hosted replay player at aexer0e.dev simulates about six times faster.

Simulation results are unchanged: across 90 test replays, the engine produced output identical to 2.0.1.
