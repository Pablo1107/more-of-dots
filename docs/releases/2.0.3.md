# More of Dots 2.0.3

- Replays that record only a map number play on more maps. Classic and experimental matches share one starting layout, so a map known in one mode now plays in the other: classic Fahero 29, 46, 47, 49 and 50, and experimental Fahero 0, Zolamare 33 to 44 and the three- and four-colour maps.
- The hosted replay player at aexer0e.dev keeps the replays you open, shows tournament brackets with their replays, makes share links, and opens replays from links such as Discord attachments.

Converting each of the 33 maps known in both modes reproduces its stored layout exactly. The replays that needed a converted layout played to their recorded ends with every order applied.
